Job description

MetaPi is looking for an LMS Administrator to manage and maintain our Moodle Learning Management System and Microsoft Teams platforms. The role involves updating courses, managing users, and supporting trainers to ensure smooth delivery of training programs.

Key Responsibilities
  • Create and update courses on Moodle
  • Upload training materials, assignments, quizzes, and assessments
  • Enroll students and assign user roles
  • Monitor course progress and generate basic reports
  • Create and manage Microsoft Teams for each training batch
  • Schedule sessions and manage meeting recordings
  • Provide basic technical support to trainers and students
  • Ensure course content is organized and up to date
Requirements
  • Bachelor’s degree in IT, Computer Science, or a related field
  • Experience working with Moodle LMS
  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Teams
  • Basic troubleshooting and problem-solving skills
  • Good organizational and communication skills
What We Offer
  • Professional working environment at MetaPi
  • Exposure to national-level training programs
  • Growth opportunity in digital learning operations
  • Salary Ranges from 40K to 45K
  • The position is based in Head Campus, Islamabad
